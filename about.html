Our Story

Placeholder text: Twin Cities Animal Rescue was founded by a small group of local volunteers who saw a growing need for animal rescue services in the metro area. What started as a foster network out of a few homes has grown into a full shelter and volunteer program serving the entire Twin Cities community.

Our Core Values

  • Compassion: Every animal is treated with patience and care.
  • Transparency: We are honest with adopters, fosters, and donors about each animal's needs.
  • Community: We believe lasting change happens when neighbors work together.
  • Accountability: We track our outcomes and share our impact openly.
