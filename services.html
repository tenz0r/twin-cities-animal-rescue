Adoption Process

Placeholder text: adopting from Twin Cities Animal Rescue is a simple, four-step process designed to match each animal with the right home.

  1. Browse available animals and submit an adoption inquiry.
  2. Meet the animal at our facility or during an adoption event.
  3. Complete a short adoption application and reference check.
  4. Finalize the adoption and take your new companion home.

    Foster Program

    Placeholder text: our foster program gives animals a safe, temporary home while they wait to be adopted. Fosters receive food, supplies, and medical support from our team for the full length of the placement.

    Foster placements typically last anywhere from two weeks to a few months, depending on the animal's needs.

    Volunteer Roles

    We rely on volunteers for nearly every part of our work. Current volunteer roles include:

    • Animal Care: Feeding, walking, and socializing animals at the shelter.
    • Transport: Driving animals to vet appointments or adoption events.
    • Events: Staffing adoption fairs and community fundraisers.
    • Administrative Support: Helping with intake paperwork and phone calls.
