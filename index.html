Our Mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue exists to rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome animals in need across the metro area. We believe every dog, cat, and small animal deserves a safe home and a family who will love them for life. Our Impact

Since opening our doors, our volunteers and staff have worked to change outcomes for animals across the Twin Cities. A few numbers that show our progress:

  • 1,200+ animals placed in loving homes since 2019
  • 85 active foster families supporting our program each month
  • 320 volunteer hours logged last quarter alone
  • 92% adoption success rate for animals in our care

How You Can Help

There are several ways to get involved with Twin Cities Animal Rescue, whether you have a few hours a month or are ready to open your home to a foster animal.

  • Adopt: Give a rescued animal a permanent home.
  • Foster: Provide a temporary, loving space while an animal waits for adoption.
  • Volunteer: Help with events, transport, or daily animal care.
  • Donate: Support food, medical care, and shelter costs.

Visit our Services page to learn more about each program, or go directly to our Contact page to sign up.
